When nerves hit

Steady before you speak

Take a quiet moment to find your footing before your next talk.

About 2–3 minutes, with grounding at your own pace. No sound; nothing is saved.

Before a presentation

  • Hold a cool glass or bottle for a moment.
  • Plant both feet and feel the floor beneath them.
  • Give your first sentence a little more time.
  • Find a friendly face to speak towards.
  • Sip water or green tea instead of coffee before a talk.

This is not medical advice. If panic or anxiety happens often or feels overwhelming, please speak to a doctor.
