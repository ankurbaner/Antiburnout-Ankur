For people whose work has lost its edges

The break is for your eyes, your back, and a glass of water. Not for more work.

Anti-Burnout counts the minutes you spend actively using your screen. After every 20 of them, a small card asks you to do one physical thing: rest your eyes, fix your posture, drink some water or move. It never adds a task, never asks a question and never needs a reply.

    How it works

    1

    It counts only the time you're using the screen

    Step away for a minute and it counts as a break. The timer starts again from zero.

    2

    Every 20 minutes, a small reminder appears

    It asks you to rest your eyes, stretch or drink water. Click Done, or Snooze to be reminded in 5 minutes.

    3

    Need a proper break? Take 5 minutes

    The 5-minute reset shows one simple activity to do away from the screen.

    Tips

    • Keep this tab open. Nothing is counted while it's closed.
    • On Chrome or Edge, click Track the whole computer so it knows when you walk away.
    • Press Pause during calls or meetings.

    Privacy

    • Everything stays in this browser. No accounts, no analytics, no trackers.
    • It only knows whether you're active or away, never what you type or look at.
    • It keeps today's numbers, the last 7 days and your settings. Clear them any time in Settings.
