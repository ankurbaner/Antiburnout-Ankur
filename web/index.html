For people whose work has lost its edges

The break is for your eyes, your back, and a glass of water. Not for more work.

Anti-Burnout counts the minutes you spend actively using your screen. After every 20 of them, a small card asks you to do one physical thing: rest your eyes, fix your posture, drink some water or move. It never adds a task, never asks a question and never needs a reply.

How it works

1

It counts active time, not open time

The clock only runs while you're using the screen. Stop for a minute and that pause counts as a break: your current stretch goes back to zero and no card is shown.

2

Every 20 active minutes, a card appears

It sits in the top-right corner and never covers the page. Click Done once you've done it, or Snooze to see it again in 5 minutes.

3

The longer you go, the more it asks

Under 45 minutes it's just your eyes. Then posture, then water and a stretch. The wording changes from card to card, so it doesn't read the same each time.

4

A 5-minute reset when you need more

Take a 5-minute reset opens a full-screen timer with one activity. Finishing it, or clicking I'm back, counts as a break and starts a fresh stretch.

The 5-minute reset

A 5-minute timer and one activity on screen. Each takes a minute or two; spend the rest of the time away from the screen, or click Try another activity. The same activity never comes up twice in a row.

    Getting the most out of it

    • Keep this tab open. Nothing is counted while it's closed. When you come back after more than a minute, that gap counts as a break.
    • Turn on notifications so a card still reaches you when you're in another tab or app.
    • On Chrome or Edge, track the whole computer. It then knows when you actually walk away, instead of assuming you're working whenever this tab is in the background.
    • Press Pause when you don't want time counted, such as during a call. Settings let you change the card interval, the break length and the snooze time.

    Privacy: what it does not do

    • It only knows whether you're active. By default that means a mouse, keyboard, scroll or touch event happened on this page, never which key or where.
    • No screen reading. No window titles, no screenshots, no clipboard.
    • No network. No accounts, analytics, trackers or third-party fonts. After the page loads, nothing leaves your browser.
    • Only a few numbers are stored, in this browser's local storage: your current stretch, today's screen time, cards done, resets taken and your settings. Clear them any time in Settings.
    • Whole-computer tracking and notifications are opt-in. Tracking uses the browser's Idle Detection permission, which reports only whether you're active or idle and whether the screen is locked.

    Prefer an app? The desktop app runs in your menu bar or system tray on macOS, Windows and Linux. It watches activity across the whole computer and shows the same cards. The 5-minute reset is adapted from Off-Duty.
